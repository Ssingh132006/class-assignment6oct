Average Output Voltage Formula

V_{dc} = \frac{2 V_m}{\pi} \cos\alpha

For a 1-phase fully controlled bridge with highly inductive continuous load. Firing angle α delays conduction, enabling voltage control from +2Vm/π down to negative values during regenerative inversion.

RMS Output Voltage Formula

V_{rms} = \frac{V_m}{\sqrt{2}} = V_{s,rms}

Under continuous conduction, the load receives the full AC waveform rectified; therefore the RMS value equals the supply RMS voltage.

Peak Inverse Voltage (PIV) & Commutation

PIV = V_m = \sqrt{2} \cdot V_{rms}

Each SCR or Diode must withstand the full peak supply voltage when reverse biased during the non-conducting half-cycle.
